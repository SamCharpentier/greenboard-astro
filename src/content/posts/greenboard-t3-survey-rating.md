Every year, the [T3/Inside Information Software Survey](https://t3technologyhub.com/live-from-t3-key-wealthtech-and-ai-findings-from-the-2026-t3-inside-information-software-survey/) asks financial advisors to rate the technology they use to run their firms. That matters because these are not opinions based on a sales demo or a short trial. They reflect the systems advisors rely on every day, including when they are trying to keep compliance work moving inside their firms.

This year's results come from **2,906 responses**, the largest dataset of its kind in the profession. Greenboard received an 8.43 average user rating, the [highest among compliance platforms in the 2026 T3 Software Survey](https://t3technologyhub.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2026-T3-Inside-Information-Software-Survey.pdf).

To put that in context, the survey authors consider anything above 8.0 "remarkable." The average rating across the category was 7.58, meaning Greenboard scored nearly a full point higher than the category average.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/67d0a8a9156b7b7bd46ffe28/69b43ef3d6166f06a6fe4c87_5421182c-41da-4657-8b23-33d97164c8c0.png)

### Greenboard is the fastest-growing compliance platform in the survey

Greenboard represents **1.45% market share in the compliance category**, up from **0.33% the year before**. That is more than **fourfold growth in a single year** and the largest year-over-year market share increase among compliance platforms in the 2026 T3 Software Survey.

The survey authors note a pattern that shows up consistently across advisor technology. Platforms with strong user ratings tend to grow as practitioners recommend them to peers. Satisfaction spreads through professional networks before market share fully catches up.

That pattern is clearly visible here. **Greenboard earned the highest rating in the category and recorded the fastest market share growth among compliance platforms** in the survey. When compliance officers find a platform that actually helps them run their program, they talk about it.

### What the survey says about compliance technology

The report does more than list scores. It also discusses where the compliance technology category is heading.

The authors specifically called out Greenboard as an early mover in **AI-enhanced compliance systems**, noting that tasks such as regulatory documentation review, communications monitoring, and repetitive compliance analysis are well suited to AI systems.

Compliance teams are being asked to do more without expanding headcount. Regulatory expectations continue to grow. At many firms, pieces of the compliance program still live across email threads, spreadsheets, and disconnected systems.

When an examiner shows up, that fragmentation creates real problems. Documentation gaps appear. Records are inconsistent. There is no clear audit trail showing how decisions were made.

That is the operational reality compliance officers manage every day. The platforms that earn the strongest ratings in surveys like this tend to be the ones that address those problems directly, rather than adding another system to manage.

### What this means if you are evaluating compliance technology

An industry survey cannot tell you whether a specific system fits your firm’s regulatory obligations, supervision structure, or internal workflows. That requires a real evaluation.

However, independent user ratings do provide a useful signal. They show which platforms practitioners trust once the software is in production, not just during vendor demonstrations.

For firms evaluating their compliance technology stack, that is a signal worth acting on.
