Amazon Web Services (AWS) is the world’s most widely adopted cloud platform, trusted by startups, financial institutions, and global enterprises to run critical workloads. Greenboard is built on AWS from the ground up. We rely on AWS to host our application, store sensitive data, and deliver a responsive, reliable experience to compliance teams who cannot afford downtime or lag. By running on AWS, Greenboard benefits from the same global infrastructure, security model, and performance characteristics that power some of the most demanding regulated organizations in the world.

For Greenboard customers, AWS translates directly into stability and scale. As your firm grows, adds more users, and increases review volume, Greenboard can scale compute, storage, and network capacity without interrupting day to day compliance work. During peak periods like quarterly attestations, AWS enables Greenboard to handle surges in tasks, uploads, and searches while keeping workflows fast and responsive. Multi region coverage and a broad footprint of availability zones help us design for resilience and uptime, so your team can rely on Greenboard as a core part of its compliance stack.

Security and governance are central to why Greenboard runs on AWS. AWS provides strong building blocks for identity and access management, encryption, logging, monitoring, and network isolation. On top of those primitives, Greenboard implements its own application level controls, permissioning, attestations, and audit trails that are tailored for financial services regulation. This layered approach aligns with how regulators expect firms to think about vendor risk and cloud usage. It gives customers a clear story about where data lives, how it is protected, and how activity is tracked over time.

AWS is also a key ingredient in Greenboard’s ability to ship features quickly and support modern compliance use cases. The breadth of AWS services for storage, databases, analytics, and artificial intelligence allows Greenboard to process large volumes of records, power fast search, generate flexible reporting, and support automation across reviews, approvals, and monitoring. As AWS continues to invest in next generation infrastructure and AI capabilities, Greenboard can draw on that innovation to keep enhancing the compliance operating system our customers rely on.

In short, AWS gives Greenboard the foundation to be secure, performant, and future ready. By pairing AWS’s cloud infrastructure with Greenboard’s domain specific compliance workflows, customers get a platform that can grow with their firm, handle changing regulatory expectations, and remain dependable when it matters most.
