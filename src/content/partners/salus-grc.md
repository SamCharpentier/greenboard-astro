Salus GRC is a modern governance, risk, and compliance advisory built to break the traditional compliance mold. We embed directly with investment managers, RIAs, private funds, and broker-dealers to run compliance as a true operating function—one that supports growth rather than slowing it down.

Our model pairs white-glove governance expertise with AI-native compliance technology to reduce risk, eliminate manual work, and remove friction across the compliance lifecycle. Veteran consultants lead the work day-to-day, supported by scalable systems that deliver clear oversight, audit-ready documentation, and defensible evidence firms can rely on as they grow. Through our partnership with Greenboard, firms gain a more connected compliance ecosystem, combining experienced leadership, modern tooling, and practical execution to meet regulatory expectations with confidence.
