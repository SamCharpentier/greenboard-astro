FirmScribe is an industry leading provider of iMessage and SMS capture for regulated firms, trusted by more than 70,000 onboarded iPhones and billions of archived messages. Built on a secure, native iOS experience, FirmScribe enables employees to keep their existing phone numbers and continue texting within the standard iMessage app, while delivering complete, compliant mobile message capture to any archive. FirmScribe has become the gold standard for financial institutions that need reliable mobile communications archiving without disrupting how employees work.

Greenboard partners with FirmScribe to ensure that every mobile message is captured and seamlessly integrated into the Greenboard archive for monitoring, retention, and supervisory review. FirmScribe delivers high fidelity message capture, and Greenboard transforms that raw data into actionable compliance oversight by applying our lexicon and AI reviews. This combination gives financial firms a complete and consistent view of communications across email, chat, collaboration tools, social channels, and mobile devices in one unified operating system. Greenboard deploys the integration across the RIAs, broker-dealers, and hybrid firms that rely on Greenboard to operate modern, audit-ready compliance programs.

Together, FirmScribe and Greenboard eliminate the longstanding compliance gap created by mobile messaging. Firms gain a secure automated archival into Greenboard’s electronic communications system, and a scalable, technology-first approach to electronic communication supervision. By combining FirmScribe’s trusted capture protocols with Greenboard’s AI-native compliance engine, regulated firms get full visibility, consistent controls, and a mobile messaging solution built for the realities of modern financial services.
