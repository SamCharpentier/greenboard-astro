Microsoft plays a central role in how Greenboard delivers secure, integrated, and high fidelity compliance workflows. We use Microsoft Azure to establish trusted, encrypted connections to the AI platforms that power Greenboard’s intelligence features. These Azure based connections ensure that model calls, data transfers, and inference operations meet the security and privacy requirements expected in financial services. Azure’s identity, network, and encryption controls give Greenboard a secure channel for handling sensitive compliance content when it is being analyzed by AI systems. This architecture helps customers adopt advanced AI capabilities with confidence and transparency.

Microsoft is also the backbone for Greenboard’s document handling. Many compliance workflows depend on accurate reviews of policies, marketing materials, disclosures, procedural documents, and presentation decks. Greenboard uses Microsoft technology to render Word and PowerPoint documents directly in the platform so users can view files exactly as they appear in their native format. This prevents misinterpretation, formatting issues, and data loss that can occur when documents are converted into alternate formats. It also allows reviewers and approvers to work efficiently without leaving Greenboard or downloading sensitive materials.

Azure provides additional benefits that support Greenboard’s performance and reliability. Its mature cloud infrastructure allows Greenboard to scale document processing, manage large file uploads, and maintain consistent rendering quality even across complex layouts, embedded media, and large multi page files. Azure’s global presence and security model complements our AWS hosted environment, giving Greenboard a flexible and resilient cloud foundation that supports secure specialized workloads.

By combining Azure’s secure AI connectivity with Microsoft’s native document rendering capabilities, Greenboard gives compliance teams a unified environment for reviewing content, generating insights, and maintaining complete control over their information. This approach strengthens data protection, simplifies daily tasks, and ensures firms can trust the accuracy and integrity of every document they review or analyze within the platform.
