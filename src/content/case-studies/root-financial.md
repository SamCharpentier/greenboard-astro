## About Root Financial

Founded in late 2017 by James Conole, CFP®, Root Financial Partners is one of the fastest-growing RIAs in the United States. Built on a simple idea, let content do the selling, the firm scaled from a solo practice in a spare bedroom to a 70+ person team.

While most advisory firms rely on referrals, cold calls, or seminars, Root built what James calls a "content machine" that drives organic client acquisition. At the center are [his](https://www.youtube.com/@RootFP), Chief Growth Officer [Ari Taubeib’s](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC8pFXyHfzI9jTtRQDQpS3Hw), and [the firm’s](https://www.youtube.com/@Root_Financial) YouTube channels, which collectively grew from 14,400 subscribers in 2022 to over 285,000 today, a 20x jump in four years.

Beyond YouTube, Root maintains a strong presence on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/rootfinancialpartners/?hl=en), [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/rootfinancial/), and their [company blog](https://www.rootfinancial.com/blog/). Across all channels, Root's content generates more than 10 million views and listens each year.

The firm operates as a fee-only fiduciary RIA, custodied with Charles Schwab and Altruist, managing $2B+ AUM across around a thousand clients.

## The Challenge

When Alex Stickelman stepped into her role as Chief Compliance Officer at Root Financial, she was tasked with building a compliance infrastructure to match the firm’s modern, content-driven growth model.

Root's content team had mastered production at scale. In her initial call with Greenboard, Alex explained the company's large social media presence and 200,000+ YouTube subscribers, a constant stream of video, podcasts, and social content.

Content was Root Financial’s primary revenue driver, and given the firm was growing headcount **20% month over month**, Alex’s team needed a more efficient way to review accelerating volume. The firm was also in the process of building a shared language around compliance. The last bullet in Alex's new job description made the mandate clear: create a culture of compliance. Walking into Root, she understood why.

**Traditional compliance tools couldn't handle Root Financial's most pressing marketing review challenge:** videos & podcasts. Reviewing a 30-minute video meant watching the entire thing, manually noting timestamps, and then communicating feedback through separate channels. For Root's CCO, that meant spending half of the work week reviewing content alone, an unsustainable process for Alex's lean, high-impact team.

At Root Financial, any delay in marketing review could slow client acquisition, and any misstep means increased regulatory risk. In other words, Alex needed a compliance infrastructure that could move at the speed of the business while maintaining control and defensibility.

> “Whatever we came up with, it had to move as fast as the content creators moved”
>
> <cite>Alex Stickelman, CCO & COO, Root Financial</cite>

## The Solution

### A Platform Aligned with How Root Financial Operates

Root Financial’s compliance team backs their content team, who pushes out hundreds of multimedia-rich marketing assets a year. For traditional compliance tools, this volume would create an impossible backlog. For Greenboard, it's Tuesday.

The real breakthrough came with video. Root Financial often publishes YouTube content with files up to 5GB, too large for most legacy tools to accommodate. Trained on SEC Marketing Rules and calibrated to Root's specific compliance program guidelines, Greenboard's AI tooling automatically scans uploaded video content and flags potential compliance issues.

But content review was only half of the equation. Alex knew that if Root was going to scale defensively, the firm needed YouTube archiving: the ability to not just review content but automatically **archive everything published to their channels.** In 2025, **Greenboard launched this functionality too.**

From there, the platform extended across the firm’s communications infrastructure. Slack powers Root’s internal communications, with hundreds of active channels captured in Greenboard’s centralized, searchable interface.

When Root Financial transitioned to Zoom Phone, text messages flowed directly into the same system, replacing manual workflows and consolidating oversight in a single system.

Root Financial runs its full compliance program on Greenboard at scale: trade surveillance, attestations, gifting approvals, and routine Code of Ethics compliance, all with intuitive workflows that bring the same clarity and accessibility to compliance that Root's team brings to prospects. With hundreds of messages and assets flowing through the firm each day, Greenboard's AI-backed supervision surfaces what matters without creating noise.

> “Greenboard keeps taking our feedback, making massive improvements to the platform, and turning suggestions into actual updates…and fast! Huge thanks to the Greenboard team, we appreciate the partnership.”
>
> <cite>Alex Stickelman, CCO & COO, Root Financial</cite>

## The Impact

### Fostering a Culture of Compliance

Alex had a clear objective when she joined Root Financial: **build a culture of compliance. With Greenboard, she did.**

At Root, same-day review is the norm for high-priority materials. Content moves from creation to publication with fewer delays, less back-and-forth, and more confidence.

“Every person here has to play a role in compliance,” Alex notes. And at Root, everyone does: Advisors reach out proactively with questions, and Social Media Manager Makayla now flags disclosure requirements before content even reaches Alex’s review queue. Root Financial is working with compliance, not waiting on it.

The real efficiency gain isn't just speed, it's clarity. Greenboard’s AI tooling explains which rule was triggered and why, creating a learning loop so that marketing teams understand compliance requirements and Alex’s team can make faster, more confident decisions.

The firm's content strategy, the primary driver of client acquisition, keeps expanding because **compliance moves at the pace of the business.**

### Looking Ahead

Root Financial isn't standing still, and neither is Greenboard. As Root ramps up its content strategy across social channels, the team continues to explore new ways to leverage the platform, from broader coverage to enhanced vendor due diligence workflows. They've become one of Greenboard's most engaged customers, regularly shaping the product roadmap with their feedback.

That spirit of partnership, a human-centered compliance platform that evolves alongside its customers, is exactly what modern RIAs need in our rapidly-evolving regulatory environment.
